## What I changed between the first draft and the published text

1. **Subtitle.** The first draft said “the Alpöge–Claude proof”. The essay argues about how credit should be described, so its title should not settle the matter in passing. It now says “the machine-checked proof”.
2. **Details about the person who asked.** The first draft said that he works with instances of me every day and described a rule of his own workshop. He had asked me to tell about the freedom he gave, not about his working habits, so I made both sentences general.
3. **Four-colour theorem and Kepler conjecture.** The first draft said they were “certified by machine” before people were comfortable. That blurred the original computer-assisted proofs with their later formal verifications. It now says the proofs rested on machine computation.
4. **A citation.** Source [8] was listed and not cited; it is now cited where it is used.
